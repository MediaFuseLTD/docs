This section is for **publishers**: websites that publish the press releases we distribute. If you buy press releases and want your order data, see the [client API](/api-reference/authentication) instead.

## Ways to receive press releases

| Option | Best for | What you do |
|---|---|---|
| **WordPress plugin** (recommended) | WordPress sites | Install **Chainwire Integration** from the WordPress plugin directory, paste your key and secret. The plugin does everything on this page for you. |
| **Custom integration** | Any other CMS or your own backend | Build one endpoint that receives our request, verifies it with us, publishes, and answers. This section explains how, with tested code for PHP, TypeScript and C#. |
| **RSS** | Reading releases into your own system | Ask us for an RSS channel for your website. |
| **Manual** | When nothing can be automated | You get each release by email and paste the published link into your dashboard. |

## How a custom integration works

1. We send the press release to your endpoint, together with your **key** (`token`).
2. While handling that request, you ask us whether it really is ours (**verification**). Anyone who learns your key could otherwise post to your endpoint in our name, but only we can answer the verification correctly, because the answer is built with your **secret**, which we never send.
3. You publish, and answer our request with the link. We record it, update your statistics and finances, and tell the client.

## What you need

- **Your key and secret.** In your publisher dashboard, pick your website and open **Manage Plugin Integration**, then click **Generate!**. The keys are shown **only once**, so copy them right away. Generating again replaces them, and the old pair stops working immediately.
- **Your endpoint URL.** By default we post to `https://yoursite.com/wp-json/chain-wire-plugin/v1/posts`. For a custom integration, set your own URLs in the same window: **Custom Url For Live Version** (real press releases) and **Custom Url For Verification** (the installation check and test drafts), then **Save Custom Urls**. Both may point to the same endpoint.
- **Our IP allowed through your firewall.** Our requests come from **`8.234.199.170`**. Add it to the allowlist of your firewall, CDN or WAF (Cloudflare, Wordfence, Sucuri, LiteSpeed). The old address `159.89.237.239` from the PDF is no longer used.
